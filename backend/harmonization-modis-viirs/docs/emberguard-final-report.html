03 · Data reality check

What the real files told us

MODIS (since 2001) has 1 km pixels; VIIRS (since 2012) has 375 m pixels and sees about 3.5× more hotspots. We learn the ratio where both overlap, test it on hidden years, and use it to convert the old MODIS years.

Data already downloaded

  • NASA FIRMS country files, Tunisia + Algeria.
  • MODIS 2001–2025 · VIIRS Suomi NPP 2012–2025.
  • 4 CSV files, 127 MB total (Algeria VIIRS is 105 MB).
  • Columns as expected: satellite N, confidence l/n/h, type.
  • The MODIS files report version 6.2: cite that.

Two traps

Gas flares: about 50% of the VIIRS “vegetation fire” detections are on Saharan oil fields (≈ 31.5°N, 5.5–6°E). Keep only north of 34°N.

Sparsity: Tunisia alone is thin (≈ 6,300 filtered MODIS detections in 25 years). Calibrate on the whole northern belt, demo on Bizerte.

Fire season, north of 34°N (VIIRS)

  • August ≈ 62,300 · July ≈ 27,500
  • September ≈ 14,600 · October ≈ 10,600
  • December (lowest) ≈ 1,400
  • Total ≈ 137,000 detections, 2012–2025
  • So: a 72-hour view in mid-November will look calm. That is honest, and we say so.

09 · Scientific guardrails

What we never claim

The blueprint's guardrails plus what our data taught us. Every screen, slide and passport follows these.

From the blueprint

  1. A hotspot is a thermal detection, not an individual wildfire.
  2. No detection does not mean “definitely no fire”.
  3. A grid aggregate alone is not harmonization. Ours is calibrated and tested.
  4. Agreement between sensors is not ground truth.
  5. No ignition probabilities, no street-level precision.
  6. No future data in any past prediction.
  7. Decision support, never automatic dispatch or official authority.
  8. Cite sources, product versions, methods and limits.

From our data

  1. Keep only north of 34°N; say why (gas flares).
  2. Pre-2012 values are a lower bound: fires too small for MODIS are lost.
  3. Converted MODIS carries more noise; show the low / high range.
  4. Months with too little data are labelled, not interpreted.
  5. Weather is “context”, never an official danger index.
  6. The AI only rephrases our numbers; the number check enforces it.
  7. Mock data is never shown as a result.

11 · Do / Don't

What we should do, and what we should not

Do

  • Finish the MUST tier before touching anything else.
  • Lead the demo with Bizerte: local impact, calibrated on the wider region.
  • Make Sensor Truth Mode flawless: it is the 10 seconds judges remember.
  • Show the hidden-year proof in the app and on slide 5.
  • Say our limits first: 34°N cut, lower bound before 2012, weather ≠ danger index.
  • Keep the template passport as the fallback for every AI call.
  • Store timestamps and sources for every number (observation, forecast issue, valid time).
  • Keep JSON backups so the demo survives a server or Wi-Fi failure.
  • Submit two hours early; test links in a private window.
  • Sleep in shifts. A rested presenter beats one more feature.

Don't

  • Don't build five half-features. One solid calendar beats five promises.
  • Don't calibrate on Bizerte alone or on 2022–2024 only.
  • Don't include fires south of 34°N.
  • Don't invent a fire-danger formula or a probability of ignition.
  • Don't start the Time Machine, ML models or multi-agent orchestration.
  • Don't let the AI write a number that isn't in our data.
  • Don't restructure the repo or change the JSON without P3.
  • Don't put API keys in the website code.
  • Don't use NASA logos, copyrighted images, or anyone under 18.
  • Don't leave after 20:00, drop below 3 on site, or exceed 7 slides.

Q&A answers to prepare

  • Why not ML? A count ratio with pooling is explainable and tested; ML is a next step against this baseline.
  • Why calibrate beyond Bizerte? Bizerte alone has too few detections for stable monthly ratios.
  • Why north of 34°N? Gas flares pass NASA's vegetation filter further south.
  • Small fires before 2012? Can't be recovered: a lower bound, shown in the app.
  • Is this a danger forecast? No. Weather context next to history; official FWI is EFFIS's job.
  • Why trust the AI? It only rephrases our numbers, and a check rejects any number it invents.
