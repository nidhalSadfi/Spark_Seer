How it works
  1. Ingest. NASA FIRMS hotspot detections: MODIS from 2001, VIIRS (Suomi NPP) from 2012. Vegetation fires only, counted per 0.5° grid cell and month (the committed data: Tunisia and Algeria north of 34°N).
  2. Calibrate. VIIRS sees more small fires than MODIS. On the years both fly (2012–2021) we fit a ratio k = VIIRS / MODIS per cell and calendar month, pulled toward the regional ratio where a cell has little data. Checked on held-out years (panel below).
  3. Harmonize. Before 2012: MODIS × k (“VIIRS-equivalent”). From 2012: the observed VIIRS count. One consistent series, 2001–2025.
  4. Flag. Each month is compared with the same month in other years (robust z-score). ▲ |z| ≥ 3 high, ▼ low, ? low confidence: a pre-2012 month whose ratio rests on fewer than 30 MODIS detections.

Why harmonize: yearly totals

MODIS alone undercounts. Before 2012 the harmonized line is MODIS × k; from 2012 it is the observed VIIRS count (dots), so the two coincide.
